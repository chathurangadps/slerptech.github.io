# Task: Add "Why Open-Source ERP?" Benefits Section

Review the existing SLERPTECH website and add a prominent homepage section explaining why open-source ERP platforms such as ADempiere and iDempiere are commercially attractive. Do not redesign the website. Reuse the existing design system, typography, spacing, cards, CTA styling and responsive patterns.

## Placement

Place the section after the ERP introduction/value proposition and before detailed ERP services.

## Heading and introduction

- H2: `Why Open-Source ERP?`
- Supporting message: `Enterprise ERP Without the Traditional Licensing Burden`
- Explain that open-source ERP gives organizations control over technology, costs, infrastructure and future direction without dependence on traditional proprietary licensing models.

## Six benefit cards

1. **No Per-User License Fees** — explain that users, departments, branches and operations can grow without traditional per-seat ERP licensing costs.
2. **No Proprietary ERP License Fees** — explain that organizations can avoid proprietary ERP application or server licensing fees and direct budget toward implementation, integration, optimization and innovation. Do not claim infrastructure is free.
3. **Freedom From Vendor Lock-In** — mention freedom to choose implementation partner, support provider, developers, hosting, cloud, infrastructure and modernization strategy.
4. **Deploy Where You Choose** — mention on-premise, private cloud, AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and suitable VPS/cloud infrastructure. Do not imply hosting is free.
5. **Global Community Support** — mention developers, implementers, functional specialists, users, contributors and technology specialists. Clarify that SLERPTECH professional services build on the ecosystem.
6. **Customize Without Limits** — mention modules, workflows, processes, reports, JasperReports, APIs, integrations, plugins, industry functions and custom applications.

## Additional value message and transparency

Add `Greater Control Over Your ERP Investment`, explaining that organizations can reduce traditional software licensing costs and focus investment on implementation, customization, integration, infrastructure, support and innovation.

Include: `Open-source removes traditional ERP software licensing costs; implementation, hosting, customization and professional support services may still involve costs.`

Add a CTA linking to the existing contact workflow, such as `Discuss Your ERP Requirements`.

## SEO and accuracy

Naturally include open-source ERP, open source ERP software, ADempiere ERP, iDempiere ERP, ERP without per-user licensing, ERP licensing costs, no vendor lock-in ERP, customizable ERP, open-source ERP implementation/consulting and ADempiere/iDempiere consulting. Avoid keyword stuffing.

Do not claim servers, hosting, implementation or support are free; do not claim zero operating cost or automatic security. Clearly separate software licensing from implementation, infrastructure and support costs.

## Responsive and quality requirements

- Desktop: three cards per row.
- Tablet: two cards per row.
- Mobile: one card per row.
- Avoid new heavy icon libraries and external dependencies.
- Preserve accessibility, semantic headings, animations and existing sections.
- Verify build, crawlable SEO text, CTA, responsive rules, no duplication, no console errors and no horizontal overflow.
