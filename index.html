WHY iDEMPIERE

Open by design.
Ready for your business.

iDempiere is a community-developed ERP, CRM and supply-chain suite. Its open-source foundation gives businesses the freedom to adapt and extend their systems.

01 / FREEDOM

Keep your options open.

Access to the source code gives you flexibility to choose how your ERP develops, without depending on one software vendor.

02 / COMMUNITY

Build on shared expertise.

Developers, implementers and users contribute to a global community that advances the platform.

03 / FLEXIBILITY

Make room for change.

An extensible architecture and application dictionary support adapting the platform to business requirements.
