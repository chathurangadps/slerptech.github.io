OPEN ERP ADVANTAGE

Why Open-Source ERP?

Enterprise ERP Without the Traditional Licensing Burden

Open-source ERP gives organizations greater control over their technology, costs, infrastructure and future direction.

Open source ERP software such as ADempiere ERP and iDempiere ERP helps businesses build powerful solutions without being tied to traditional proprietary licensing models.

01 / SCALE

No Per-User License Fees

Grow without purchasing additional ERP licenses every time you add an employee or user. Add users, departments, branches and operations without traditional per-user ERP licensing costs.

Scale users without traditional per-seat ERP licensing.
02 / LICENSE

No Proprietary ERP License Fees

Avoid traditional proprietary ERP application or server licensing fees and invest more of your budget in implementation, integration, optimization and business innovation.

Direct ERP investment toward business value.
03 / FREEDOM

Freedom From Vendor Lock-In

Choose your implementation partner, support provider, development team, hosting provider, cloud platform, infrastructure and long-term modernization strategy.

Your ERP strategy should not depend on one software vendor.
04 / DEPLOY

Deploy Where You Choose

Deploy ADempiere or iDempiere on-premise, in a private cloud, on AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, or suitable VPS and cloud infrastructure.

Cloud, private cloud or on-premise—the choice remains yours.
05 / COMMUNITY

Global Community Support

Benefit from a worldwide ecosystem of developers, ERP implementers, functional specialists, business users, contributors and technology specialists sharing knowledge and improvements.

SLERPTECH adds professional implementation and support to the open-source ecosystem.
06 / CUSTOMIZE

Customize Without Limits

Adapt modules, workflows, business processes, JasperReports, APIs, plugins, third-party integrations, custom applications and industry-specific functionality.

Adapt the ERP around your business—not your business around the software.

GREATER CONTROL

Greater Control Over Your ERP Investment

Open-source ERP can reduce traditional software licensing costs while giving organizations greater control over their platform, infrastructure, integrations and future modernization strategy.

Focus investment on areas that create business value through open-source ERP implementation, customization, integration, infrastructure, professional support and innovation. SLERPTECH provides ADempiere consulting and iDempiere consulting across that lifecycle.
